Scope and hypothesis
====================
Question:
  Can the installed arm64 Open MPI application exchange data with two ranks
  locally and under the PC-210 Slurm PMIx v6 direct-launch path, and are MPI
  tasks, steps, allocation resources, and processes recovered after cancel?

Hypothesis:
  Because Open MPI, the application, and the Slurm PMIx plugin all load the
  verified Homebrew PMIx 6.1.0 stack, local PRRTE launch and Slurm
  `srun --mpi=pmix_v6` launch should both complete broadcast, all-reduce, and
  point-to-point exchange. Cancelling after those operations should remove both
  task PIDs and release the allocation.

Fixed conditions:
  ranks=2
  node=PC-210 only
  cpus-per-task=1
  memory=256M
  bcast value=42
  all-reduce input=rank; expected sum=1
  ring send value=1000+rank
  success time limit=1 minute
  cancel time limit=2 minutes

Out of scope:
  Ubuntu package/configuration changes, multi-node MPI, daemon restart,
  production configuration/plugin changes, and mpirun inside a Slurm
  allocation.
