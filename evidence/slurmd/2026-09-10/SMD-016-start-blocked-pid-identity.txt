The original preflight used one condition for two different failures:
1. launchd PID and pidfile value differ
2. kill -0 on the launchd PID fails
Both paths emitted "identity mismatch". The post-failure readback proves the
stored identities currently match, so the message did not establish an actual
PID mismatch. The liveness predicate and diagnostic must be separated.

Record launchctl output, service state, launchd PID, pidfile bytes/value, and ps
command separately. Require launchd state=running, equal numeric PIDs, and the
production slurmd command. Do not collapse kill -0 failure into PID mismatch.
