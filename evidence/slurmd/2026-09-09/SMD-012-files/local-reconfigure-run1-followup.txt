Harness correction after reviewing the controller journal:

The controller emitted `different slurm.conf` both for the alternate worker
configuration and after the production worker configuration was restored.

differs from the controller configuration, this warning alone cannot prove a
failed restoration. The driver now stores matching worker log lines in
`worker-config-hash-warnings.txt` instead of failing the test on their presence.
The byte-for-byte local backup comparison remains mandatory.
